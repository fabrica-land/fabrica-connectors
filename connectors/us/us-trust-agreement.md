This Trust Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into by the Grantor through the creation of a Property Token. The identifying information of this trust (the "**Trust**") may be found in the Property Token to which this Agreement is attached.

### How This Trust Works (Non-Operative Summary)

*This summary is provided for the reader's convenience only. It is not an operative part of this Agreement, creates no rights or obligations, and shall not be used to construe any operative provision. If this summary and any operative provision differ, the operative provision controls.*

- A trust holds recorded legal title to a specific piece of real property, exactly as real-property law provides. Where applicable law treats a trust as a relationship rather than an entity, that title is held by the trustee in a fiduciary capacity for the trust (or as local law otherwise requires).
- The entire present beneficial interest in that trust is personal property, and it belongs to whoever rightfully holds the Property Token — or, while the token sits inside a smart-contract facility such as a loan pool, escrow, or bridge, to the person this Agreement's rules designate (usually the depositor, or a buyer who assented to a financed purchase). Transfer the token to a new rightful holder and the beneficial interest — with all its rights of possession, use, income, and disposition — passes to that holder automatically, under this Agreement.
- The token is not the land, and it is not the deed. It is the record that controls the trust. Record title to the land itself moves only by a recorded deed, and real-property law (liens, taxes, recording priority) always governs the land.
- The holder directs the trustee. By default the holder is also the trustee, and no platform or company has any key, approval right, or management role in the trust.
- If someone sends the token to your wallet without your agreement, you presumptively own the beneficial interest from the moment of receipt — including for taxes and the other burdens of ownership — and can use every ownership right immediately. You can refuse the interest entirely by an onchain act (ordinarily by sending the token back, or by burning it); a valid refusal erases that ownership retroactively to the day you received it, to the extent the law allows. Some laws — including federal tax law — honor the refusal fully only if you act promptly after learning of the transfer, so refuse quickly. But moving, selling, listing, or using the token in any way other than sending it back or burning it counts as permanently accepting the property — backdated to the day you received it (exceptions: a listing you cancel, or that expires, without any sale does not count; nor do acts needed merely to preserve the token or the property while you decide; and where sending it back and burning it are both impossible, you can still refuse by recording your refusal onchain — after which you can hold the token only as a custodian for its rightful owner).
- A thief gains nothing by stealing the token. But if a thief sells it to an innocent buyer who pays value and takes control without notice of the theft, that buyer may keep the token and the beneficial interest, and your remedy is against the thief. Secure your keys, and act immediately on any theft.
- If the land is lost by operation of law (tax sale, foreclosure of a recorded lien, condemnation), the trust dissolves, the token becomes void, and the token holder keeps the wind-up rights to any proceeds. If the holder dies, whoever lawfully succeeds to control of the wallet keeps operating immediately, without waiting for a court — acting for whoever inherits under estate law; merely finding someone's keys does not make access lawful. The beneficial interest passes as personal property under the law governing the holder's estate. If token keys are lost, or no one lawfully succeeds to key access, this Agreement provides recorded, contestable procedures to unwind the trust. Burning the token is permanent and can never be undone: done by the owner, it is the first step of taking the land out of the trust by deed (done by a recipient who never accepted, it is a refusal, as described above).
- Lending against the token is personal-property secured lending under UCC Article 9 against the token and, where expressly granted, the beneficial interest; it is never structured as a mortgage of the land itself.

### Recitals

- Whereas blockchain Smart Contracts provide a secure and interoperable way of representing ownership of digital assets through the use of an electronic ledger to immutably track, document, and verify all transactions. As a consequence, new systems and methods are emerging to store, interact with, and transact on real and digital assets.
- Whereas the grantor wants to link ownership of real property to a Property Token so as to benefit from digital operations and services.
- Whereas on issuance of the Property Token, the grantor will attach this Agreement to the Property Token, and intends for this Agreement to act as the trust agreement governing the trust that holds the Property. For the purposes of interoperability and standardization, the grantor and any subsequent party to this agreement intend to keep all the identifying information of this Agreement stored in the Property Token to which this contract has been attached. All the operations, definitions, and procedures related to the Property Token and the Trust will be stored in this Agreement.
- Whereas the purpose and design of this Trust allow the beneficiary to use blockchain technology to facilitate transfer and utilization of property ownership. To the extent provided in this Agreement, the trustee's power and authority to deal with the Property are subject to the direction of the beneficiary, who holds all present economic and possessory rights to the Property, subject to the contingent equitable interests of determinable future beneficiaries and legal successors. The beneficiary (who may change from time to time and is at all times the person determined under **Section 7** of this Agreement, which may differ from the person having custody or control of the Property Token) is entitled to income, maintains the sole right of termination, and is intended to be treated as the owner of the Property for federal, state, and local tax purposes to the maximum extent applicable tax law permits.
- Whereas the parties intend, in the tradition of the American land trust, that the entire beneficial interest in the Trust is personal property — an interest in the Trust, and not an estate or interest in land — freely assignable by the digital mechanics of this Agreement, while title to the Property itself remains held, transferred, and encumbered only under the real-property law of the jurisdiction where the Property is located.
- Whereas the parties intend that beneficial ownership of the Trust, and the validity and effect of operations concerning the Property Token, be determinable from the record of the Blockchain Network read together with this Agreement, subject only to mandatory law (including sanctions law and judicial process) and to instruments recorded in the real property records as this Agreement provides, and they adopt **Section 4.3** to that end.
- Whereas the parties intend to treat all valid operations done via blockchain on the Property Token as final as among all persons bound by this Agreement, with the same force and effect as a fully executed agreement; the effect of such operations on persons not bound by this Agreement is determined by applicable law, including Article 12 of the Uniform Commercial Code where enacted, as described in **Section 11**. Further, the parties intend to treat digital signatures with the same legal consequence as wet signatures.

### 1. Trust Purpose ###

1. The purpose of the Trust is to create a legally enforceable link such that ownership of the Property Token determines ownership of the Property, so as to allow for time-efficient, cost-effective, accurate, and secure ownership, transfer, use, and enjoyment of real property title in the digital environment (the "**Trust Purpose**").
2. On creation, the Trust will take and hold title to the Property, and ownership of the Trust will be represented by ownership of the Property Token. Regardless of the sale, hypothecation, or other transfer of the Property Token, title to the Property will remain in the Trust until such time as either the then-present Beneficiary (or another authorized party) distributes the Property out of the Trust, or record title is transferred out of the Trust by operation of law as described in **Section 10.5**. To facilitate the Trust Purpose and maintain the integrity of the token-property relationship, the Trustee shall not execute or deliver any deed or other agreement transferring the Property out of the Trust unless and until (a) the Property Token has been burned through the `burn` or `burnBatch` functions of the Token Contract, making it permanently untransferable, or (b) the dissolution is authorized under **Sections 10.2, 10.3, or 10.4** of this Agreement or required under the perpetuities savings provision of **Section 12.5**.
3. Subject to **Sections 6 and 7**, the owner(s) of the Account or Smart Wallet holding the Property Token shall be the Beneficiary of the Trust and have the right to appoint the Trustee. All rights and obligations in this Agreement shall at all times be linked to the owner of the Property Token, provided that such ownership is established through a valid Account or Smart Wallet, and not merely through temporary custody by a Functional Contract. Rightful ownership (as distinct from temporary custody) of the Property Token confers full present beneficial ownership of the Property, including all economic and possessory rights, subject to the contingent equitable interests of other Beneficial Interest Holders as defined in **Section 2.26**.
4. **Nature of the Beneficial Interest.** The entire beneficial interest in the Trust — comprising the Owner Rights of the present Beneficiary and every contingent interest of the other Beneficial Interest Holders (collectively, the "**Beneficial Interest**") — is, and is hereby declared by the Grantor and agreed by every person who at any time claims any interest under or through this Agreement or the Property Token to be, **personal property**. No Beneficiary or other Beneficial Interest Holder holds, by virtue of the Beneficial Interest, any legal or equitable estate in the Property as land; the Beneficiary's rights in respect of the Property — including the rights described in **Section 6.1** — are held under, and exercised through, the Trust and this Agreement, and are in no way diminished by this characterization. This characterization is a term of the Trust fixed at its creation, in the tradition of the American land trust, and controls whenever the characterization of the Beneficial Interest is at issue. References in this Agreement to a transfer, assignment, disclaimer, holding, encumbrance, or vesting of the Beneficial Interest mean the present beneficial interest of the Beneficiary — exclusive of the contingent interests of the other Beneficial Interest Holders, which are neither transferred nor extinguished by any such event and continue in the class as **Sections 2.26 and 12.5** provide — except where this Agreement expressly refers to the interests of the Beneficial Interest Holders collectively. Accordingly:
   - (a) the Beneficial Interest passes by assignment under **Section 7** upon each valid transfer of beneficial ownership, without a deed; no transfer of the Property Token, and no assignment of the Beneficial Interest, is a conveyance of an interest in land; and, to the extent any applicable law requires an assignment of the Beneficial Interest to be made by a signed writing, the electronic records and signatures described in **Section 4.1**, together with this Agreement, satisfy that requirement;
   - (b) a security interest in the Property Token or in the Beneficial Interest — in each case to the extent expressly granted by the debtor in an authenticated security agreement or equivalent signed electronic record satisfying UCC § 9-203 (see **Section 11.2**) — attaches, is perfected, has priority, and is enforced under Article 9 of the Uniform Commercial Code as personal-property collateral, and not under the law governing mortgages of, or foreclosure upon, real property (see **Sections 11.2 and 11.7**); and
   - (c) succession to the Beneficial Interest on the death of a Beneficiary is governed by the law governing intangible personal property and by **Section 7.6** of this Agreement.

   The operative mechanism that transfers the Beneficial Interest is the assignment effected by this Agreement itself upon each valid transfer of beneficial ownership under **Section 7**, of which the Property Token is the authoritative record and instrument of transfer; **Section 11.7** states the relationship between that assignment mechanism and the treatment of the Property Token as a controllable electronic record under **Section 11**. Nothing in this **Section 1.4** limits **Section 11.6**: the Property itself is and remains real property, title to which is held, transferred, and encumbered only in accordance with the real-property law of the jurisdiction where the Property is located.
5. **Integrity of the Trust Corpus.** It is a principle of this Agreement that the corpus of the Trust is fixed at the creation of the Trust: the corpus consists of the Property as described at that time (**Section 2.12**), together with the legal appurtenances and accessions of, and the proceeds arising from, that Property under applicable law and this Agreement, and of nothing else. For as long as the Property is operated through the Property Token, no direction of any Beneficiary, no act of the Trustee, and no instrument of any third party may substitute any other asset for the corpus, add any asset to it, or convey any part of it out of the Trust (**Section 1.2**); the Trustee shall refuse, and has no power to accept, any purported standalone addition to the Trust — including any deed or other instrument by which any person, including a stranger to this Agreement, purports to convey any other property, and whatever liabilities attach to it, into the Trust — and any such purported addition is void as to the Trust, vests nothing in it, and imposes no duty, debt, tax attribute, or liability on the Trust, the Trustee, or any Beneficial Interest Holder (**Section 8.1.2**). The only exceptions are those effected by mandatory law and the events described in **Section 10**, each of which leaves a recorded trace. Dealings that this Agreement authorizes — including leases and encumbrances of, and other dealings with, the Property under **Sections 6.1 and 8.1**, the collection and distribution of income and proceeds to the Beneficiary under **Sections 6.1 and 10.7**, and every transfer, pledge, custody, or other operation concerning the Property Token or the Beneficial Interest under **Sections 7 and 11** — are exercises of rights in or through the corpus, not alterations of its composition; and the way interests in the Property arise and bind the Trust's title under the real-property law of the jurisdiction where the Property is located (**Sections 11.6 and 11.8**) is unaffected by this Section. Any ambiguity concerning the composition of the corpus, or concerning the effect of any act or instrument upon it, shall be resolved in favor of the principle stated in this Section. Upon dissolution of the Trust, this principle is released in full: nothing in this Section limits the freedom of the Beneficiary, or of any other person entitled under **Section 10** to direct conveyance, to direct the deed of distribution in any lawful manner, including a conveyance to one or more grantees and a division of the Property, or of distinct rights in it, among them.

### 2. Definitions ###

1. **Account** means an Externally Owned Account (EOA) on a Blockchain Network, which is an Address controlled directly by one or more private keys held by individuals or entities.
2. **Address** means a public key address on a Blockchain Network.
3. **Beneficiary** means the individual(s) or entity(ies) holding present beneficial ownership of the Trust and the Owner Rights as determined under **Section 7**. Beneficial ownership is ordinarily established by control of the Account or Contract Account holding the Property Token, but may differ from the current onchain holder due to Functional Contract custody (**Section 7.3(b)**), a Qualified Designation during such custody (**Section 7.3(c)**), custodial holding (**Section 11.3**), succession on death (**Section 7.6**), disclaimer of an unsolicited Transfer (**Section 7.7**), unauthorized transfers (**Section 7.5**), or alternative dissolution upon loss of token control (**Sections 10.2 through 10.5**). The Beneficiary is one of the Beneficial Interest Holders, but the term "Beneficiary" as used in this Agreement refers to the present interest holder unless otherwise indicated. In the event the Property Token has not yet been minted, or has been minted but has not yet been owned by any Account, the Beneficiary shall be the original Grantor. In the event ownership of the Property Token is fractionalized (as discussed further in **Sections 6.1, 6.3, and 7.3**), then the defined term Beneficiary as used in this Agreement shall apply to the various owners of the Property Token as a group.
4. **Blockchain Network** means any distributed ledger technology network used to maintain records of ownership and management of the Property Token to which this Agreement is attached. The relevant blockchain network may change due to actions of the Beneficiary, including the wrapping of the Property Token to a different blockchain network or the minting of the Property Token on a different blockchain network.
5. **Confirmed Transaction** means a transaction that has been recorded on the Blockchain Network which is considered "finalized." A transaction on Ethereum is considered finalized when that transaction becomes part of a block on the chain with a supermajority link between two checkpoint blocks. Other Blockchain Networks maintain similar or different definitions of Confirmed Transactions that should be adhered to in the event of the transfer or minting of the Property Token on an alternative Blockchain Network aside from Ethereum.
6. **Contract Account** means an Address controlled by a Smart Contract. A Contract Account may either: (a) serve as a method of holding Property Tokens under the direct control of specific individuals or entities who thereby become Beneficiaries (a "**Smart Wallet**"), or (b) operate as a functional contract (such as escrow, bridge, or collateral management) where custody does not automatically confer beneficial ownership (a "**Functional Contract**").
7. **Creation Date** means the date and time at which the Trust was created by the Grantor.
8. **Ethereum** means the Ethereum mainnet and the consensus blockchain for such mainnet (networkID:1, chainID:1) as recognized by the official Go Ethereum Client implemented at https://github.com/ethereum/go-ethereum as of the Creation Date.
9. **Grantor** is the individual or entity who creates the Trust and grants the Property to the Trust.
10. **Mint** means a Confirmed Transaction `mint()` or `mintBatch()` function on the Token Contract that results in the association of a Token ID to an Address.
11. **ERC-1155 Token (Token)** means a digital asset developed based on the ERC-1155 multi-asset standard, and tracked by the Token Contract on the Blockchain Network with a unique identification code (Token ID). In the event the Property Token to which this Agreement is attached references an alternative Blockchain Network, such Property Token may follow a different Token standard, or utilize a different definition of Token.
12. **Property** is the bundle of rights identified in the legal description stored in the Token Metadata under the fields `definition.claim` and `definition.offchainRegistrar`. The Property so described is the bundle of rights which is to be deeded into the Trust and held throughout the life of the Trust.
13. **Property Token** means a Token issued using the Token Contract. In this case, such Property Token should be assumed to reference the token and standard on the Blockchain Network on which that Property Token is tracked. In the event a Property Token is wrapped, or transferred, to an alternative Blockchain Network, the Property Token will reference the ultimate controlling interest Token at any present moment.
14. **Redeem** means the result of a Confirmed Transaction of the `burn` or `burnBatch` functions on the Token Contract that results in the permanent locking or removal of the Property Token from circulation. In the event the Property Token has been wrapped, or transferred, to an alternative Blockchain Network, it may be required to unwrap, or transfer back, the Property Token to the original Blockchain Network to execute the `burn` or `burnBatch` functions. Depending on which Blockchain Network the Property Token was minted, the specific function names may be different, but Redeeming such token shall refer to effectively locking the Property Token so that it becomes untransferable forever into the future.
15. **Smart Contract** means the bytecode deployed on a specific Blockchain Network Address which acts as a program to execute and run a series of processes or interactions.
16. **Token Contract** means the Smart Contract used to maintain records of ownership and management of the Property Token to which this Agreement is attached.
17. **Token ID** means the unique and immutable identifier determined on Trust creation and assigned to the corresponding Property Token. The Token ID is generated based on the digital signature of multiple fields from the Token Metadata, including the precise language of this Agreement.
18. **Token Metadata** means the data directly stored within the Property Token as well as external data stored on IPFS (or similar immutable storage system) and linked within the Property Token itself (using the fields `definition` and `configuration`). These will always include Token ID, Trust Name (stored as `definition.holdingEntity`), and Property legal description (`definition.claim`). Other additional information may be included to simplify property identification, verify past ownership and other activities. Alternative Blockchain Networks may utilize different data configurations or definitions.
19. **Transfer** means any operation performed through the Token Contract that assigns the Property Token to a new non-null Address.
20. **Trustee** means the individual or entity appointed by the Beneficiary as the Trustee of the Trust. At creation of the Trust, the Trustee may also be appointed by the Grantor.
21. **Trust Name** means the name of the Trust, more particularly defined in the Token Metadata (in `definition.holdingEntity`). It is recommended to encode the Token ID in the Trust Name using Base32 Crockford encoding.
22. **Alternative Dissolution Event** means a dissolution of the Trust under **Sections 10.2, 10.3, 10.4, or 10.5**, or upon the Perpetuities Termination Date under **Section 12.5**, without prior Redemption of the Property Token.
23. **Notice of Lost Token Control** means a sworn affidavit (or other sworn recordable instrument permitted by applicable law), in recordable form, notarized in the form (acknowledgment, jurat, or equivalent) required by the recording statutes of the jurisdiction where the Property is located, recorded with the relevant county recorder or equivalent recording authority in that jurisdiction, declaring that the Beneficiary (or their legal successor) has lost the ability to Redeem the Property Token (or, in the case described in **Section 10.2.7**, that the Property Token has been Redeemed by another by way of disclaimer, rendering Redemption by the Beneficiary impossible). The Notice shall identify: the full legal description of the Property (matching the legal description stored in the Token Metadata under `definition.claim`) and the assessor parcel number where the county uses one; the Trust Name exactly as the Property is vested of record (and the name of the record titleholder, if stated differently of record); the recording reference (instrument number or book and page) of the deed by which the Property was conveyed into the Trust; the name of the claimant; a mailing address at which the claimant, and the Trustee if different, will receive contest notices and deliveries under **Section 10.2.2**; the Token ID, the Token Contract address, the Blockchain Network, and the last known Account or Contract Account holding the Property Token. The Notice shall describe the circumstances of loss, shall state that, to the best of the claimant's knowledge, the Property Token is held by an Account or Smart Wallet at the time of recording (or the disclaimer-Redemption statement described in **Section 10.2.7**), and shall request indexing against both the record titleholder (the Trust) and the claimant.
24. **Quiet Period** means a period of ninety (90) calendar days commencing on the date a Notice of Lost Token Control is recorded with the relevant county recorder or equivalent recording authority, during which any interested party may contest the Notice or assert a competing claim to the Property or the Property Token.
25. **Void Token** means a Property Token that has been dissociated from the Property and the Trust following an Alternative Dissolution Event. A Void Token confers no beneficial ownership rights, no Owner Rights, and no interest in the Property, regardless of its continued existence on the Blockchain Network. For the avoidance of doubt, while a Void Token may continue to exist as a controllable electronic record under UCC Article 12, it shall confer no rights under this Agreement except as expressly provided in **Sections 10.5.2 and 10.5.4**.
26. **Beneficial Interest Holders** means, collectively, (a) the present Beneficiary, (b) any person who would become a Beneficiary upon a valid Transfer of the Property Token under the objective mechanics of **Section 7** (including transfer to an Account under Section 7.1, transfer to a Smart Wallet under Section 7.3(a), explicit assignment of Owner Rights under Section 7.3(b)(ii), or a Qualified Designation under Section 7.3(c)), and (c) any successor who would acquire a beneficial interest through the succession provisions of **Sections 7.6 and 10.3**, including any Key Successor and the persons who would take under the law governing a deceased Beneficiary's estate. The interests described in clauses (b) and (c) are genuine, defeasible contingent equitable interests in the Trust, arising simultaneously with the creation of the Trust and determined by the objective transfer and succession mechanics of this Agreement rather than by the identity of any specific future holder. The inclusion of future and contingent interests in this definition does not confer any present right of action, possession, or control upon any person other than the present Beneficiary, except for the narrow procedural standing to petition for appointment of a Continuity Trustee under **Section 12.6** following a judicial determination of merger and the Beneficiary's failure to designate within the time period specified therein (standing that may be exercised only by a person claiming a present interest or an interest by succession or designation, not by a stranger asserting only the possibility of a future transfer). This definition does not alter the tax treatment of the Trust under **Section 12.7**.
27. **Beneficial Interest** has the meaning given in **Section 1.4**.
28. **Key Successor** means the person who, after a Beneficiary's death, lawfully succeeds to control of the Account or Smart Wallet holding or controlling the Property Token — whether under the deceased Beneficiary's estate plan or lifetime arrangements, under applicable succession law, or under the recovery or succession features of a Smart Wallet. A person who obtains access through theft, compromise, misappropriation, or other unauthorized means is not a Key Successor and is governed by **Section 7.5**.

### 3. Establishing the Trust

1. The process for entering into this Agreement is as follows:
   1. The Grantor, or a third party instructed by the Grantor, creates the Trust by generating a Token ID using the function `generateId` on the Token Contract;
   2. the Grantor transfers the Property into the Trust by deed, or instructs a third party owner to do so, and records the deed with the relevant authority or recorder. The deed language shall specify both the Token Contract address and the Token ID, and shall name the grantee using the Trust Name stated in the Token Metadata (`definition.holdingEntity`) verbatim (in the trustee-of formulation where the conventions of the recording jurisdiction require it — see **Section 9.1**); the deed may also recite that the Trustee has full power and authority to convey and otherwise deal with title to the Property as this Agreement provides, and the absence of that recital does not affect the validity of the conveyance;
   3. the Grantor, or a third party instructed by the Grantor, Mints the Property Token through the Token Contract. This Agreement is attached to the Token Metadata via the `operatingAgreement` field. A copy of the conveyance deed may be attached to the Token Metadata to facilitate validation, but is not required for the Trust to be legally effective;
   4. Upon a Confirmed Transaction, the Beneficiary will be entitled to the Owner Rights (described below). Ownership records for the Property Token will be kept by the Token Contract.
2. The Trust is not intended to be, shall not be deemed to be, and shall not be treated as a general partnership, limited partnership, joint venture, corporation or joint stock company. The Trust is revocable by the Grantor until the Property Token is Minted: if the Property has been conveyed into the Trust and the Mint fails or does not occur, the Grantor may revoke the Trust and recover title. Upon such a pre-Mint revocation, the Trustee (or the Grantor acting as deemed Trustee) shall execute, and the Grantor may record, a reconveyance deed in recordable form reciting the revocation under this Section 3.2; the recorded reconveyance evidences the revocation of record. Upon the Mint, the Trust becomes irrevocable for all purposes, and the Beneficiary thereafter retains the sole right to distribute the Property and dissolve the Trust as provided in **Section 10**. The Mint is the single, objective irrevocability event; **Sections 5.1 and 10.8** are to be read consistently with this rule.
3. If the Property has been transferred into the Trust but the Property Token has not yet been minted and no Beneficiary has been specified, the Grantor shall be considered the Beneficiary of the Trust, unless otherwise specified.

### 4. Transactions and Interactions with Token Contract

1. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, as between and among the Grantor, the Trustee, each Beneficiary and Beneficial Interest Holder, and every other person bound by, or claiming any right under or through, this Agreement or the Property Token: (a) any signature or execution made through the use of private keys on a Blockchain Network for any matters relating to the Property Token or the Trust, where made with intent to authenticate or adopt the operation or record to which it relates, shall be valid, sufficient, and final, as if signed in writing; and (b) any other signed electronic record relating to the Property Token or the Trust — including a record of a person's assent made offchain in connection with an operation described in this Agreement — shall be valid and enforceable according to its terms. The parties intend each such signature, execution, and record to constitute an effective electronic signature and electronic record under the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (15 U.S.C. § 7001 et seq.) and applicable enactments of the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (in California, Cal. Civ. Code § 1633.1 et seq.), attributable to the person who made or authorized it and logically associated with the record it authenticates. The effect of any onchain act on a person who is not so bound is determined by applicable law: as to the Property Token and the Beneficial Interest, by **Section 11.4** and UCC Article 12 to the extent applicable; and, as to record title to the Property, by **Section 11.6** and the real-property law of the jurisdiction where the Property is located. Nothing in this Section 4.1 purports to make any onchain act binding on a stranger to this Agreement by contract alone.
2. Any action that the Beneficiary or Trustee takes with respect to the Property or the Trust shall be invalid unless first instructed through a Confirmed Transaction on interaction with the Token Contract and under the conditions of this Agreement, except for: (a) actions taken pursuant to an Alternative Dissolution Event under **Sections 10.2, 10.3, 10.4, or 10.5** or a termination under **Section 12.5**; (b) ministerial preservation acts by a suspended Trustee, and acts by a Continuity Trustee, under **Section 12.6**; (c) any act that this Agreement expressly permits or recognizes as effective when made by a signed record satisfying **Section 4.1**, including a Trustee appointment or resignation under **Section 7.1**, an express acceptance under **Section 7.7.4(b)**, and the selection or acceptance of a Continuity Trustee under **Section 12.6**; (d) ministerial preservation acts of a continuing prior Trustee under **Section 7.1** or of an interim officeholder under **Section 7.6.1**, none of which requires a contemporaneous Confirmed Transaction; (e) the Beneficiary's exercise of the beneficial enjoyment described in **Section 6.1** — including possession, use, occupancy, leasing, and the collection and application of income and receipts — to the extent the act does not purport to transfer or encumber the Property Token or the Beneficial Interest, to transfer or encumber record title to the Property, or to dissolve the Trust; and (f) the Trustee's performance of the Trustee Duties under **Section 8** pursuant to a direction, instruction, or authorization given as this Agreement provides — including the preparation, execution, delivery, and recording of documents under **Sections 8.1.3 and 10** — where the Confirmed Transaction or other authorization that this Agreement requires for the underlying act has occurred.
3. **Onchain Determinability; Good-Faith Reliance.**
   1. It is a principle of this Agreement that beneficial ownership of the Trust, and the validity and effect of operations concerning the Property Token, are determinable from the record of the Blockchain Network read together with this Agreement, subject only to: (a) mandatory law that this Agreement cannot vary, including sanctions law, insolvency law, and judicial process; (b) instruments recorded in the real property records, as described in **Sections 10.5 and 11.8**; (c) the limited records that this Agreement itself identifies as determinative of particular questions (**Sections 7.3(c), 7.6, 10.2 through 10.4, and 11.3**); and (d) the offchain facts that this Agreement itself makes relevant — whether a Transfer was authorized (**Section 7.5.1**), whether a Transfer was solicited, and whether acceptance occurred by offchain conduct (**Section 7.7.4**) — as to which the onchain record governs in favor of good-faith reliance under **Section 4.3.2**. The provisions of this Agreement shall be construed in favor of this principle.
   2. A designation, Transfer, or other operation that is valid on the record of the Blockchain Network as read with this Agreement is conclusive in favor of a person who in good faith and for value acts, takes, finances, or otherwise relies on it (or who, without acquiring an interest, materially changes position in reasonable reliance on it, to the extent of that change of position). A transferee asserting an Onchain Disclaimer bears the burden of establishing that the Transfer was unsolicited; a Transfer shown by any signed record or onchain act to have been bargained for or agreed to is conclusively solicited.
   2. **Onchain Disclaimer.** A transferee of an unsolicited Transfer who has not accepted the Beneficial Interest under clause 4 may refuse the Beneficial Interest in whole (an "**Onchain Disclaimer**") by any of the following, each of which the parties designate as a signed record delivered to the Trustee and the transferor by its recording on the Blockchain Network, no offchain instrument, filing, or further delivery being required:
      - (a) **Return.** A Confirmed Transaction transferring the Property Token back to the Address from which it was received, or to another Account or Smart Wallet controlled by the transferor (where a transferor-controlled destination is identifiable, it is the preferred destination). This is the ordinary disclaimer. A good-faith return under this clause (a) is not an unauthorized Transfer by, and creates no liability of, the disclaimant to the true Beneficiary or any other person, even where the sending Address proves not to have been the true Beneficiary's — the remedies of **Section 7.5** run against the wrongdoer; and the disclaimant's selection among the receiving Address and other transferor-controlled destinations is a mode of return, not a direction of disposition within the meaning of clause 3.
      - (b) **Disclaimer Burn.** A Redeem of the Property Token executed before any acceptance under clause 4 and not in connection with any redemption demand, direction to the Trustee, or other exercise of Owner Rights (a "**Disclaimer Burn**"). A Disclaimer Burn is an irrevocable refusal of the whole Beneficial Interest; it is not an exercise of Owner Rights and is not a Dissolution Event or an instruction to convey under **Section 10.1**. Upon a Disclaimer Burn, the transferor (or the transferor's successor) is the continuing Beneficiary of a Trust whose Property Token has been destroyed, and may restore control of the Property and effect conveyance through **Section 10.2** (as modified by **Section 10.2.7**) or **Section 10.4**. Whether a Redeem was executed in connection with a redemption demand, direction, or other exercise of Owner Rights is determined by the signed records satisfying **Section 4.1**: production of a signed demand or direction made at or before the Redeem conclusively establishes the Redeem as an exercise of Owner Rights under **Sections 7.7.4 and 10.1**, and not a Disclaimer Burn.
      - (c) **Recorded refusal (fallback).** Only where both return and Redemption are impossible, are prohibited by applicable law, or are not reasonably available to the transferee, a Confirmed Transaction originated by the transferee's Account or Smart Wallet that records, in a form retrievable from the Blockchain Network and logically associated with the Property Token, the transferee's unequivocal refusal of the whole Beneficial Interest. A disclaimant proceeding under this clause (c) who continues to hold the Property Token holds it thereafter as a mere custodian for the rightful Beneficiary, without Owner Rights and without duties other than to refrain from dealing with the Property Token and to permit and reasonably cooperate with its retrieval; any later purported exercise or Transfer by the disclaimant is unauthorized and governed by **Sections 7.5 and 11.4**.

      An Onchain Disclaimer must refuse the entire Beneficial Interest received in the disclaimed Transfer; no partial disclaimer is permitted. Nothing in this Section precludes a disclaimant from also making a disclaimer satisfying the form, content, filing, or delivery requirements of an applicable statutory disclaimer regime; the parties intend an Onchain Disclaimer to operate as such a disclaimer to the maximum extent applicable law permits.
   3. **Relation back.** An Onchain Disclaimer is effective retroactively to the moment of the disclaimed Transfer, under this Agreement and to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law: the Beneficial Interest is treated as never having vested in the disclaimant; the disclaimant is treated as never having been a Beneficiary or Trustee; and the incidents of ownership arising under this Agreement — including the responsibilities and entitlements of **Section 6.1** and the tax attribution of **Section 12.7** — are attributed as if the disclaimed Transfer had not occurred, so that the transferor (or, if the transferor has died, the transferor's successors under **Section 7.6**) remained the Beneficiary throughout. The transferor's succession arrangements under **Section 7.6** are unaffected by the disclaimed Transfer. An Onchain Disclaimer is a refusal to accept; it is not a transfer, assignment, or release of the Beneficial Interest by the disclaimant, and the disclaimant may not direct its destination. Relation back does not discharge the limited custodial duties stated in **Section 7.7.2(c)**: a disclaimant by recorded refusal who continues to hold the Property Token remains bound, for as long as that holding continues, to refrain from dealing with the Property Token and to permit and reasonably cooperate with its retrieval, notwithstanding that the disclaimant is treated as never having been a Beneficiary or Trustee; those duties are the only obligations under this Agreement that survive a valid Onchain Disclaimer. This relation back binds every person bound by this Agreement; as against persons not so bound it operates to the extent applicable law gives it effect, and it is in every case subject to mandatory law — including federal and state tax and tax-collection law, any statutory disclaimer regime of applicable law that conditions a disclaimer's effect on its being made within a reasonable or prescribed time (in California, Cal. Prob. Code § 279), sanctions law, judicial sales, insolvency and creditor law, and the finality of instruments recorded in the real property records — and to **Sections 4.3 and 11.4**. Relation back does not extend or revive any statutory refund, appeal, or limitation period.
   4. **Acceptance; conclusive and retroactive to receipt.** The disclaimer right terminates upon the transferee's acceptance of the Beneficial Interest, which occurs upon the earlier of: (a) any voluntary exercise of Owner Rights by the transferee — including any Confirmed Transaction originated by the transferee's Account or Smart Wallet that transfers, lists, encumbers, lends against, fractionalizes, or otherwise deals with the Property Token; any direction to the Trustee; any redemption demand, including a Redeem executed in connection with a demand or direction that the Trustee convey the Property, which is acceptance and preserves in full the transferee's right to conveyance under **Section 10.1**; and any other exercise of rights under this Agreement — other than an act described in clause 2, an act reasonably necessary to preserve the Property Token or the Property pending refusal, or a listing or offer that is cancelled or expires without resulting in any Transfer or other consummated dealing, which is not acceptance, the disclaimer right subsisting notwithstanding it (a listing that results in a Transfer is acceptance, and **Section 4.3.2** protects any person who in good faith and for value relied on the listing); or (b) the transferee's express acceptance by Confirmed Transaction or other signed record satisfying **Section 4.1**. Acceptance is conclusive and confirms the vesting described in clause 1 retroactively to the moment of the Transfer: the transferee is, and is treated as having been from receipt, the Beneficiary for all purposes of this Agreement. An acceptance evidenced by a Confirmed Transaction is conclusive as to all persons. Conduct occurring offchain — including taking possession or use of the Property or receiving or directing its income — is likewise acceptance, but as against a person acting in good faith on the record of the Blockchain Network, **Section 4.3** governs, and acceptance and disclaimer status may be determined by such a person from the record of the Blockchain Network alone. A Transfer that the transferee solicited, initiated, or agreed to acquire — including every purchase, exchange, settlement, Qualified Designation under **Section 7.3(c)**, and other bargained-for acquisition — is accepted upon the Transfer itself, and no disclaimer right exists with respect to it.
   5. **Coordination; limits.** The following limits and coordinating rules apply:
      - (a) **Trustee office.** No person becomes Trustee by reason of an unsolicited Transfer before acceptance (**Section 7.1**); the prior Trustee's continuation in office is limited to ministerial preservation.
      - (b) **No assumption of personal obligations.** No Transfer, of itself, imposes on any transferee personal liability on any contract, covenant, or debt of the transferor or of any prior holder; obligations that attach to ownership or possession of the Property under applicable law are governed by that law and by the presumptive attribution of **Sections 6.1 and 12.7**, subject to retroactive divestment by a valid Onchain Disclaimer (**Section 7.7.3**) and confirmed by acceptance (**Section 7.7.4**).
      - (c) **Sanctions.** The vesting, disclaimer, and relation-back rules of this Section operate subject to applicable sanctions and similar mandatory law; where such law applies, a Transfer to, or a dealing by or on behalf of, a sanctioned or blocked person may be ineffective, blocked, or subject to licensing requirements notwithstanding any provision of this Section.
      - (d) **Operative Instruments; tax sale.** If an Operative Instrument is recorded under **Section 10.5** while a disclaimer right subsists, Section 10.5 applies according to its terms, the person entitled being determined after giving effect to any valid Onchain Disclaimer as **Section 10.5.2** provides. A tax sale or other involuntary transfer that terminates the Trust under Section 10.5 is the terminal resolution of an unsolicited Transfer that was never discovered or disclaimed; nothing in this Section prevents or delays that operation of law.
      - (e) **Protected parties; theft.** An Onchain Disclaimer does not limit the rights of a qualifying purchaser or protected secured party under **Section 11.4** or of a person protected under **Section 4.3**, and does not affect **Section 7.5** (a Transfer resulting from unauthorized access or use is governed by Section 7.5 whether or not disclaimed).
      - (f) **Death of the transferor or transferee.** If a transferor to whom a disclaimed interest reverts has died, **Sections 7.6 and 10.3** apply to the transferor's position as they would for any Beneficiary, and the death of a transferor or of a presumptive transferee before a disclaimer is not, by itself, an acceptance and does not terminate the disclaimer right; a disclaimer made on a deceased or incapacitated transferee's behalf by the transferee's legal successor or personal representative, to the extent applicable law permits, is not an exercise of Owner Rights.

### 10. Trust termination

1. The process for dissolving the Trust is as follows ("**Dissolution Event**"):
   1. first, the Beneficiary will burn the Property Token by executing the `burn` or `burnBatch` functions on the Token Contract;
   2. second, after a Confirmed Transaction evidencing the burn, the Trustee will execute and deliver a deed in recordable form (including notarization as required by applicable law); the deed shall include a statement signed by the last controller of the Property Token by a signature satisfying **Section 4.1**, authorizing the specific transfer, except where this Agreement provides a substitute for that signature (**Sections 10.2.5(d), 10.4, 10.5, and 12.5**); the deed shall name as grantee the Beneficiary or the person designated in the redemption demand or the Beneficiary's direction; and, where the grantee is the Beneficiary, the deed shall recite that the grantee is the present Beneficiary in whom the entire present beneficial interest is already vested, so that the conveyance effects no change in beneficial ownership (the absence of that recital does not affect the validity of the conveyance); and,
   3. the Trustee will transfer the Property out of the Trust.

   A Redeem executed by the then-present Beneficiary otherwise than as a Disclaimer Burn does not change that Beneficiary's position: the Beneficiary remains the Beneficiary notwithstanding the destruction of the Property Token (Section 2.3 notwithstanding), and remains entitled to direct conveyance under this Section 10.1 at any time.
2. If the Beneficiary is unable to Redeem the Property Token due to loss of access to the private key(s) or signing credentials controlling the Account or Smart Wallet, the Trust may be dissolved through an Alternative Dissolution Event. This Section applies only when the Property Token is held by an Account or Smart Wallet at the time the Notice of Lost Token Control is recorded, except as provided in **Section 10.2.7**. If the Property Token is held by a Functional Contract and cannot be returned to an Account or Smart Wallet through the Functional Contract's ordinary onchain operation, the Beneficiary shall proceed under **Section 10.4**. If the relevant county recorder or equivalent recording authority refuses to accept the Notice of Lost Token Control for recording, the Beneficiary shall proceed under **Section 10.4**. The process is as follows:
   1. The Beneficiary shall record a Notice of Lost Token Control with the relevant county recorder or equivalent recording authority in the jurisdiction where the Property is located. Within ten (10) calendar days of recording, the Beneficiary shall deliver a copy of the recorded Notice by certified mail, return receipt requested, to: (a) the Property address as shown in the records of the county assessor or tax collector and, if different, the assessee or taxpayer mailing address shown on the county tax roll; where those records show no situs address for the Property (as is common for unimproved land), delivery to the assessee or taxpayer mailing address of record satisfies this clause (a); (b) each person who appears in the official records of the county recorder as the holder of a recorded lien, deed of trust, mortgage, or other encumbrance against the Property, at the address shown in the recorded instrument or, if more current contact information is reasonably available to the Beneficiary, at such current address; and (c) each secured party of record under any financing statement filed against the Beneficiary as debtor and covering the Property Token or the Beneficial Interest, as disclosed by a search of the applicable UCC filing office of the Beneficiary's location, at the address shown in the financing statement. Deposit of the copy of the recorded Notice in the United States mail, certified, return receipt requested, postage prepaid, and addressed as this Section requires constitutes delivery for purposes of this Section and **Section 10.2.4(c)**, whether or not the mailing is actually received. Within the same ten (10) calendar days, the claimant shall also cause a Confirmed Transaction, which may be originated from any Account, directed to the Account or Contract Account holding the Property Token (or, in the case described in **Section 10.2.7**, to the Address that executed the Disclaimer Burn), identifying the recorded Notice in a form retrievable from the Blockchain Network (the "**onchain notice leg**"). For purposes of clause (b), the Beneficiary's obligation extends to instruments appearing in a search of the grantor-grantee index (or equivalent official index) maintained by the county recorder; the Beneficiary is not required to obtain a full title search or title report. Failure to notify a party whose interest does not appear in the official records or filing office described above does not invalidate the Notice or the Quiet Period. The Quiet Period shall commence on the date of recording.
   2. The Notice of Lost Token Control, once recorded, is intended to impart constructive notice under applicable recording statutes of the Beneficiary's claim; the direct-delivery and onchain-notice duties of **Section 10.2.1** apply whether or not the recording statutes give the Notice that effect. Any party with an interest in the Property Token or the Property may contest the Notice or assert a competing claim during the Quiet Period by recording with the same county recorder any instrument permitted by applicable law that gives constructive notice of the contest, including but not limited to a lis pendens, a notice of pending action, an affidavit of adverse claim, or any equivalent instrument recognized by the recording statutes of the jurisdiction where the Property is located. A Notice of Lost Token Control recorded by a different claimant with respect to the same Property Token also constitutes a contest. A contest is likewise effective if, before expiration of the Quiet Period: (i) an interested party tenders such an instrument for recording, the recorder refuses it, and proof of the tender together with a copy of the instrument is delivered to the Trustee and to the claimant at the address stated in the Notice; or (ii) legal proceedings contesting the claim are filed and written notice of them (including a copy of the filed pleading) is so delivered. Subject to the preceding sentence, a contest must appear in the county records before expiration of the Quiet Period; the filing of legal proceedings alone, without a corresponding recorded instrument or the delivery described above, does not constitute a contest for purposes of this Section. If a contest is made during the Quiet Period, the process under this **Section 10.2** shall immediately halt and the matter shall be resolved under **Section 10.4**.
   3. If a Confirmed Transaction is initiated by the Account or Smart Wallet holding the Property Token during the Quiet Period, the process under this **Section 10.2** shall immediately halt. For purposes of this Section:
      - A Confirmed Transaction includes any transaction signed by or originating from the Account or Smart Wallet holding the Property Token, not solely a Transfer of the Property Token.

   6. Upon recording of the Deed of Distribution, the Property Token shall become a Void Token.

The mechanism is layered, and the parties adopt each layer expressly:
   1. **The Property Token.** A qualifying purchaser acquires its rights in the Property Token free of a claim of a property right in the Property Token, as UCC § 12-104(e) provides. This power exists only in favor of a purchaser who establishes control, the giving of value, good faith, and the absence of notice of an Adverse Claim, in each case determined as of the time the purchaser obtains control (**Section 11.4.6**); it does not validate the wrongdoer's ownership, excuse the wrongdoer, or limit **Section 7.5** as to any transferee who does not satisfy those conditions.

   4. **Protected secured parties.** A party who obtains a security interest in the Property Token for value, in good faith, without notice of an Adverse Claim (**Section 11.4.6**), and who obtains control of the Property Token as required by applicable law (a "**protected secured party**") shall receive the same protection under this Agreement as a qualifying purchaser to the extent permitted by applicable law.    5. **Allocation.** Where a qualifying purchaser or protected secured party prevails, the prior Beneficiary's remedy is against the party who effected the unauthorized Transfer, not against the qualifying purchaser or protected secured party. This allocation is deliberate: it preserves the prior holder's ownership against thieves, donees, and transferees with notice (**Section 7.5.1**), while giving good-faith purchasers for value the finality that makes the Property Token reliable in commerce; as between the two innocent parties, the loss falls on the holder responsible for the security of the compromised credentials (**Section 7.5.2**), whose recourse is against the wrongdoer. 8. **Priority Framework: the Land Lane and the Token Lane.** Interests in the Property, on the one hand, and interests in the Property Token and the Beneficial Interest, on the other, arise and travel in two distinct legal lanes. The Beneficiary's interest is at all times an interest in the Trust (**Section 1.4**): the token lane controls everything the Trust has; the land lane determines what the Trust has.
   3. **The crossing gate.** The event that carries a land-lane outcome into the token lane is a recorded instrument. When an instrument effecting a transfer of the Trust's title by operation of law or legal process is recorded, **Section 10.5** converts the token-side position accordingly: the Trust dissolves as to the Property, the Property Token becomes a Void Token, and the token-side claim converts to the wind-up authority and proceeds entitlement of **Section 10.5.2** and the reinstatement eligibility of **Section 10.5.4**.    4. **Applications.** Without limiting the foregoing: (a) a lien or encumbrance validly recorded against the Property has priority, as to the Property, over any later token-side pledge or Transfer; the later pledgee or transferee acquires the Beneficial Interest, or a security interest in the Property Token or Beneficial Interest, in a Trust whose title is so encumbered, and if the recorded lien is foreclosed and the resulting instrument recorded, the token side holds the **Section 10.5.2** rights in any surplus; (b) a purchaser of the Property Token acquires the Beneficial Interest in a Trust whose title is exactly what the land records and applicable real-property law make it — the protections of **Section 11.4** run against competing claims to the Property Token and the Beneficial Interest, never against interests in the Property; and (c) whether an unrecorded land-side claim binds the Trust's title is determined by the recording acts and other applicable real-property law, which this Agreement neither strengthens nor weakens.
   5. **Court residual.** Judicial resolution under **Sections 10.4 and 12.4** remains available for the cases this framework does not answer mechanically, including: competing claims to beneficial ownership of the Property Token itself (**Section 7.5.4**); contests recorded during a Quiet Period (**Section 10.2.2**); challenges to the validity of an Operative Instrument (**Section 10.5.3**); disputes concerning assent to a designation (**Section 7.3(c)**), solicitation or disclaimer of a Transfer (**Section 7.7**), or death and successor identity (**Sections 7.6 and 10.3**); and the status of the Property Token under **Section 11.1**.

Matters governed by UCC Article 12 are governed by the local law of the Property Token's jurisdiction, which **Section 11.1** expressly designates as the State of California pursuant to UCC § 12-107(c)(1); under UCC § 12-107(e), that law applies even if a matter or transaction bears no relation to California.
